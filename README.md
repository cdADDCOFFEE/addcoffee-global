## Signature Drink: Spring at Huanhua Creek
Crafted with premium matcha, house-made osmanthus honey and a hint of rose essence, this signature drink layers vibrant green and soft pink hues to mirror the blooming spring by Huanhua Creek. Every sip balances earthy tea notes with delicate floral sweetness, bringing a taste of Chengdu’s poetic riverside charm to your cup.
